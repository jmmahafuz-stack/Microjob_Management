About Our Platform

MJMS (Micro Job Management System) is a home maintenance service management platform that connects customers with verified, company-trained professionals for a wide range of services including:

  • ✓ Plumbing Services - Expert plumbing solutions for all your needs
  • ✓ Carpentry Services - Quality woodwork and furniture repair
  • ✓ AC Repair Services - Professional air conditioning maintenance and repair

To provide a seamless, transparent platform that connects homeowners with skilled, verified professionals, making home maintenance simple, affordable, and reliable.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted home services marketplace, where quality work is guaranteed, customers are protected, and professionals are empowered.

Our Values

Trust, transparency, quality, and customer satisfaction drive everything we do. We believe in fair pricing and honest service.
